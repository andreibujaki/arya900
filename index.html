FAQ

Short answers — more in docs/faq.md.

Is Arya 900 open source?

No. The product is proprietary. This site and GitHub repo publish downloads and legal text only — not application source.

Is Free really free?

Free is for personal, non-commercial light use under the EULA. Commercial or organizational use needs Pro or Business when those tiers are available.

Why does Windows warn me?

The current Free installer is not code-signed. SmartScreen or company policy may warn or block it until a signed build ships.

Can I activate Pro inside the Free installer?

No. The Free build is Free-only. A full Pro-capable installer will ship when Pro goes public.

Do my chats leave my PC?

For normal Free use, chat is designed to stay on-device. Checkout or license validation may use the network when you use those features. See the Privacy notice.

Where do I get support?

Use GitHub Issues for bugs, or the release page for version questions. Contact details appear in the Privacy notice.
