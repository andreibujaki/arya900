# FAQ

## Is Arya 900 open source?

No. The product is **proprietary**. This repository publishes downloads and legal text only. See [LICENSE](../LICENSE) and the [EULA](../legal/EULA.en.md).

## Is Free really free?

Free is for **personal, non-commercial light use**. Commercial or organizational use requires Pro or Business when those tiers are available. See the EULA.

## Where do I download?

Always use the official GitHub Release:  
https://github.com/andreibujaki/arya900/releases/latest

## Why does Windows warn me?

The current Free installer is **unsigned**. SmartScreen or company security policy may warn or block it. A signed build is planned later.

## Can I unlock Pro in the Free installer?

No. The Free build is compile-time Free-only. Pro/Business will use a separate full installer (or later entitlement flow) when those tiers go public.

## Do my chats leave my PC?

For normal Free use, chat and local model work are intended to stay on your device. See [Privacy](../legal/PRIVACY.en.md) for what may leave the PC (e.g. checkout or license validation when used).

## Which languages are supported in legal docs?

EULA, Privacy, and Subscription terms are published in **English** and **Romanian** under [`legal/`](../legal/).

## How do I report a bug?

Open a GitHub Issue using the bug template and include Windows version, Free app version, and steps to reproduce.
